We tend to picture burnout as something dramatic — a breaking point, a wall you hit all at once. In practice, it's usually quieter than that. It creeps in through small, easy-to-explain-away signs, long before anything feels like a crisis.

Here are five of the quieter ones worth paying attention to.

## 1. You're tired, but wired

You get to the end of the day exhausted, and yet lying down to sleep, your mind won't stop moving. This mismatch — bone-tired but mentally switched on — is one of the clearest early signs that your nervous system hasn't had a proper chance to downshift in a while.

## 2. Small things feel disproportionately hard

Replying to a message, making a decision about dinner, having a minor conversation — things that would normally take no effort start to feel like a lot. This isn't a character flaw. It's usually a sign your reserves are lower than you realise.

## 3. You've stopped noticing how you feel

Not stressed exactly, not fine exactly — just running on autopilot, several steps removed from your own body. If someone asked "how are you, really?" and you'd have to stop and think, that's worth noticing.

## 4. Rest doesn't feel restful

A weekend off, a lie-in, a quiet evening — and somehow you still don't feel replenished. Passive rest (scrolling, watching, half-resting while still half-working) isn't always the same as your body actually getting to settle.

## 5. You're relying on stimulation to get through the day

More caffeine, more noise, more to-do list ticking just to feel functional — often a sign the tank is closer to empty than it looks from the outside.

## Where reiki fits in

None of these signs need a dramatic fix. Often what's missing is simply an hour where nothing is required of you — no performing, no producing, nothing to manage. A reiki session offers exactly that: a quiet, held space to let your body properly downshift, even if just for sixty minutes.

It won't solve the underlying causes of burnout on its own. But as one part of taking rest seriously, it's a good place to start.
